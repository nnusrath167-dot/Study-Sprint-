StudySprint Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

StudySprint is operated by nnusrath167-dot. This policy explains what information the StudySprint web app and installed app handle, why it is used, and how to request deletion. StudySprint is intended for people aged 13 and older and is not designed for children under 13.

Information handled

  • Account information: your email address and optional display name, handled by Supabase Authentication.
  • Study information: tasks, focus-session records, goals, routines, sleep records, and app preferences. This information is stored in this browser and is synchronized to your private Supabase account when you sign in.
  • Files: files remain in this browser unless you choose to upload them while signed in. Uploaded files and their names, types, sizes, and timestamps are stored in your private Supabase account.
  • Technical information: Render and Supabase may process connection, browser, and security information needed to deliver and protect the service.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to authenticate your account, save and synchronize your study data, store files you choose to upload, and operate and secure StudySprint. Supabase provides authentication, database, and storage services. Render hosts the app and backend. The app loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive standard connection information from your browser. StudySprint does not sell personal information or include advertising.

Storage and security

Study data stored in the browser uses browser storage. Signed-in cloud records are protected with per-user database and private-storage policies. Network connections use HTTPS. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so avoid uploading highly sensitive documents and use a unique password.

Retention and deletion

Browser-only information remains on your device until you remove it or clear this site's browser data. Cloud account information, study records, and uploaded files are retained until you delete your account. Supabase may retain encrypted backups under its standard operational retention schedules.

To delete your account and associated cloud data, sign in to StudySprint, open Profile, choose Delete my account, and confirm your email and password. The request removes your cloud files, account, and synchronized records; StudySprint also clears its local study data and saved files from the browser completing the request. You can also read the account deletion instructions. If self-service deletion is unavailable, contact nnusrath167@gmail.com.

Your choices and contact

You can use browser-only features without creating an account; cloud sync and private cloud files require sign-in. You may withdraw browser notification permission through your browser settings. For privacy questions or account deletion help, contact nnusrath167@gmail.com.

This policy may be updated when StudySprint's data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when that happens.

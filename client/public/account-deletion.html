Delete your StudySprint account

You can delete your account from the StudySprint web app without contacting support:

  1. Open StudySprint Profile and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Choose Delete my account, enter the account email and password again, then confirm permanent deletion.
  3. The request deletes the Supabase account, synchronized study data, and private cloud files. StudySprint also clears its local study data and saved files from the browser completing the request.

Deletion is permanent. If the in-app deletion request is unavailable or fails, contact nnusrath167@gmail.com from the account email for help. Do not send your password.

See the StudySprint Privacy Policy for details about data handling and retention.
